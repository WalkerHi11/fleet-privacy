Privacy Notice

Tidy Town: Pack & Restore

Effective September 5, 2026 · DizzyWalk Games · Android package com.dizzywalk.tidytown

Tidy Town stores puzzle progress locally, uses analytics, ads, and attribution only with consent, and verifies purchases through Google Play.

What we do not collect

Tidy Town does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, precise location, or payment-card or bank credentials. Google Play handles payment credentials for purchases.

Identifiers and local game data

The game creates a persistent pseudonymous player ID and a persistent install ID. These IDs associate local game state, consented telemetry, and purchase verification. Core puzzle progress and restoration choices can run locally on your device.

Optional analytics, advertising, and attribution

Analytics, advertising, and attribution are default-deny. If you grant in-game consent, gameplay events and pseudonymous IDs may be sent to the Tidy Town telemetry relay and PostHog. Consented AppLovin MAX and Tenjin services may process your device advertising ID and attribution information, such as campaign and install data.

Advertising providers can share identifiers, app and ad interactions, and device or app performance signals with their advertising partners for ad delivery and measurement. Service providers also process data on our behalf.

You can change or revoke analytics and advertising consent in Settings. Revocation clears the local telemetry queue and sends opt-out signals to the applicable SDKs. It does not retrospectively erase records already received by a service.

The game may also handle crash logs and technical diagnostics to operate and improve the game.

Purchases and randomized equipment

For a purchase or restoration, the game sends the Google Play purchase token, product SKU, player ID, and install ID to its receipt-verification service. These verification records are needed even when optional analytics and ads are off. The service keeps a hash of the purchase token together with order, grant, and history records to support entitlement verification and restoration.

Paid gems and keys can be used for randomized equipment deliveries. The current odds and guarantee counters appear before each draw. Excellent-or-better equipment is guaranteed within 10 draws and S-grade equipment within 50 draws.

Local removal, records, and requests

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game removes local app data from that device. It does not delete telemetry or purchase-verification records already sent to server services. Those records may be retained to support access, restoration, refunds, and fraud prevention. Contact us about access or deletion requests using the address below.
