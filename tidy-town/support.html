Tidy Town Support

Tidy Town: Furniture Jam · DizzyWalk Games

Need a hand?

Email support@dizzywalk.com with your device model, game version, and what happened. For a puzzle issue, include the level number and a screenshot if possible.

A purchase has not arrived

Keep the game installed, reconnect to the internet, and reopen it so a pending purchase can finish verification. A payment awaiting store approval cannot deliver its items until approval is complete.

If the store shows a completed charge but the items are still missing, send us the product name, purchase date, and store order or transaction number. Do not send payment-card details, your store password, or a verification code.

Restore purchases

Use the same store account that made the purchase and the game's Restore Purchases action to check eligible subscriptions and permanent or unexpired access. Restoring access does not grant another copy of consumable coins or boosts. Contact support if an unfinished purchase was made on a device you can no longer use.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel renewing subscriptions through your platform store: Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. One-time passes do not renew automatically. The purchase screen states the applicable period and price.

For a refund request, use Apple's purchase support or Google Play's refund request. The store determines eligibility.

Game progress

Progress is saved on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove it; purchase restoration does not promise to restore puzzle progress or spent consumables. Contact us before resetting your game if you need help recovering a purchase or diagnosing a save problem.
