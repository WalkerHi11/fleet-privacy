Empire Heat — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-12 · Last updated: 2026-11-16

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game Empire Heat (also titled OMERTA, package com.dizzywalk.empireheat) on Google Play and related platforms. This page states only what is true of the Android build: every service listed below is compiled into that build.

Short version.
  • Your local settings, audio preferences, and scheduled local reminders are stored on your device.
  • The Android app uses our game server (empire-heat-api), Google Play Billing for in-app purchases, AppLovin MAX with mediated ad partners for advertising, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent in regulated regions.
  • Analytics, attribution, and diagnostics run through PostHog, Firebase / Google Analytics (plus Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications), Tenjin, Fleet Replay (masked session replay), and Sentry (crash reporting). We do not sell your personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Settings and local copies of your game: audio levels, haptics, reduced motion, and a local cache of your empire.
  • Notification schedule: local reminders are scheduled on your device by Android.

What the game sends, and to whom

  • Dizzywalk game server (empire-heat-api): random account ID (shown in Settings as your Syndicate ID), boss nickname, game actions/state, and Google Play purchase records (product, order ID, purchase token) to run the game and verify purchases.
  • Google Play Billing: processes your in-app purchases; Google handles payment and we never receive card details.
  • AppLovin MAX & mediated ad partners: AppLovin SDK (with AppLovin Quality Service), AppLovin Exchange, Meta Audience Network, Pangle / ByteDance, Liftoff / Vungle, Mintegral, InMobi, Moloco, DT Exchange / Fyber, ironSource, Chartboost, and Bigo Ads. Receives your device's resettable advertising ID and app-set ID, device model, OS and app version, IP address (approximate location), ad views and taps, SDK diagnostics, and our random player ID to deliver, frequency-cap, and measure ads and prevent ad fraud.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): collects your consent choices and region at launch in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland before ads, analytics, replay, or attribution start.
  • PostHog: receives fixed gameplay events (including purchases and ad revenue), random install ID and player ID, device/OS/app version, connection type, country (IP GeoIP), and campaign attribution properties.
  • Firebase & Google Analytics (plus Firebase Cloud Messaging): receives Consent Mode v2 state, random user ID, count-only gameplay and value-tier milestones, and push registration/timezone topic subscriptions when notifications are permitted.
  • Fleet Replay (fleet-replay.onrender.com): captures low-resolution masked still frames of the game screen every few seconds (with boss-name entry, Syndicate ID footer, and subscription terms blacked out, and paused during ads, Play checkout, or consent forms) alongside accepted gameplay events to find UI defects and crashes. Turning analytics off stops Fleet Replay.
  • Sentry: receives crash and error reports (message, stack trace, app version, session health, random player ID) at launch across regions to keep the app stable.
  • Tenjin: receives advertising ID, app-set ID, Google Play install referrer, device/OS, IP address, random player ID, count-only milestones, and verified purchase records for install attribution.
  • Google Play Games Services & Google Play In-App Review: optional Play Games sign-in profile binding and Google's native rating prompt.

The app declares Android's advertising-ID permission (AD_ID) so AppLovin MAX and Tenjin can read the advertising ID. You can reset or delete that ID anytime in your Android device settings. Provider policies: AppLovin Privacy, Google Privacy, PostHog Privacy, Sentry Privacy, and Tenjin Privacy.

Consent and your choices

In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before ads, analytics, session replay, and attribution start; crash reporting runs to keep the game functional. Outside those regions, these services start at launch. You can review or withdraw choices anytime via Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent; withdrawing analytics stops PostHog and Fleet Replay and rotates your analytics ID.

Delete my data

Email privacy@dizzywalk.com with your Syndicate ID (found at the bottom of Settings). We delete your server account and request deletion across PostHog, Fleet Replay, Sentry, Tenjin, Firebase, and AppLovin where applicable. Purchase records may be retained where legally required. Uninstalling removes local device state.

Children's privacy

Empire Heat targets a Teen rating (simulated crime themes, no gore) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
