FOODCHAIN — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-10-19 · Last updated: 2026-10-19

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game FOODCHAIN: Food Web Battler (the "Game", package com.dizzywalk.foodchain) on Google Play.

Short version.
  • No account, no login, no email. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through AppLovin MAX (rewarded videos you choose to watch, and interstitial, banner and app-open ads) and offers in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads or ad measurement use your data; you can change it later from the Game's settings.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information the Game uses

  • A random install identifier created on your device the first time you open the Game. It is the same value in our analytics, our ad platform and our attribution provider, so we can count installs, sessions, purchases and ad views without knowing who you are.
  • Gameplay and app events: screens opened, runs started and ended, nights held, ads requested and shown (with the ad revenue the ad network reports), purchases started and completed (product id, price and currency, and a one-way hash of the order id), app version, device model and OS, country, and how many sessions you have played.
  • Advertising identifier and device data used by the ad and attribution SDKs below, subject to your consent choices and your device's ad settings.
  • Purchase receipts from Google Play, checked on your device so the items you buy are granted exactly once.
  • Crash reports (stack traces, device and app version).

Service providers

  • AppLovin MAX serves ads and reports ad revenue. It uses your advertising identifier, IP address and device information to deliver and measure ads.
  • Google: the consent message (User Messaging Platform) in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and Google Play Billing for purchases.
  • Tenjin measures which ad campaign brought an install and the value of purchases and ads, using your advertising identifier.
  • PostHog receives the gameplay and app events above, keyed by the random install identifier. It never receives your name, email, contacts or advertising identifier.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, used only to fix bugs.

Your choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can review or withdraw consent at any time from the privacy option in the Game's settings. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings, and you can clear the Game's data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on your device. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of information about you; contact us to exercise them.

Children

FOODCHAIN is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Retention and security

Analytics and crash data are kept only as long as needed to run and improve the Game, then aggregated or deleted. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email kristine@dizzywalk.com.

Changes

If the Game changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
