GRAFT — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-12 · Last updated: 2026-07-12

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game GRAFT (the "Game") on Google Play and related platforms. We built GRAFT to be fun without harvesting your personal life. This page describes exactly what the Game does and does not collect.

Short version.
  • No account and no login — you can play without giving any personal information.
  • We collect only anonymous, aggregated usage analytics — never your name, email, or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data and show no third-party advertising.

Information we collect

The Game collects anonymous product analytics via PostHog when enabled, limited to gameplay events, an anonymous randomly-generated device id, session length, and device / OS / app-version. It is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect personal information

The Game has no account system and no login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any government identifier. You can play without providing any personal information.

Third-party services

The Game uses the following service providers only for the purposes described. We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

  • Anonymous product analytics via PostHog. Limited to app-usage events (screens opened, features used, session length), a randomly-generated anonymous device identifier, and basic device / OS / app-version information. IP addresses are not stored (IP discarded at ingest). This data is never linked to a real identity.

Online features

The optional online ranked ladder and shared 'colossus' build codes transmit only your in-game build and result data with an anonymous, randomly-generated player id. Sharing a build creates a code/link containing only game data — never your name, email, or contacts.

Children's privacy

GRAFT features biomech monster combat and is intended for teens and older; it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and security

On-device data lives only on your device until you remove it. Any anonymous analytics we process is retained only as long as needed to understand how the Game is used, then aggregated or deleted. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit (encryption via HTTPS) and at rest.

Your choices and rights

Because the Game stores most data on your device, you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. Where we process any anonymous analytics, you may contact us to ask what is held or to request deletion. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to processing of information about you; contact us to exercise them. We honor Google Play's Data Safety declarations for the Game.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at kristine@dizzywalk.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
