Request deletion of your SCRAPWARD account

You can ask us to delete your anonymous purchase account and associated server data by emailing michael@dizzywalk.com. You do not need to log in or have a working game connection to start a request.

Open deletion request email

This opens a draft in your email app. It does not send an email or delete data. Requests are reviewed and fulfilled manually. If no email app opens, send your request directly to michael@dizzywalk.com with the subject SCRAPWARD account deletion request.

Help us locate your records

  1. In the game, open Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES → REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION.
  2. Choose COPY REQUEST IDENTIFIERS, then paste the purchase account ID and install ID into your email. These identifiers help locate records; an identifier alone is not proof of ownership.
  3. If the identifiers are unavailable or you already uninstalled, you can still email us. An approximate installation date and device model may help locate your records. If you made a purchase, a Google Play order ID is optional additional information.

We may ask for additional information to locate the records and verify your request. Never send your private SWR purchase-recovery code, authentication token, payment details or full purchase receipt. A purchase or order ID is not required to initiate a request.

Server data and retained records

Your request covers the purchase account and associated data we hold, including its notification registrations and identifiable gameplay or diagnostic records processed for us. We review the request, identify the matching records and remove eligible data from our systems or request removal from our service providers as applicable.

Some transaction records may need to remain for tax and accounting obligations, fraud prevention or legal disputes. We retain those records only as needed for those purposes. We explain any applicable exception when responding to your request. Ad partners may hold data independently; their contact links are in the privacy policy.

Local progress and Google Play purchases

Your campaign progress, game wallet and settings are stored on your device. A server deletion request does not erase that local save. To remove it, use Android Settings → Apps → SCRAPWARD → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the game. This permanently removes local progress and consumed items; this version has no campaign cloud backup. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not itself delete server records.

Account deletion can affect your ability to recover purchases through that purchase account. It does not cancel or refund a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately in Google Play.
