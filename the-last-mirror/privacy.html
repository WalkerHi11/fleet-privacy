THE LAST MIRROR — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-22 · Last updated: 2026-07-22

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game THE LAST MIRROR (the "Game") on Google Play and related platforms. We built THE LAST MIRROR to be fun without harvesting your personal life. This page describes exactly what the Game does and does not collect.

Short version.
  • No account and no login — you can play the campaign fully offline without giving any personal information.
  • Your progress stays on your device — in this version we transmit no gameplay analytics off your device.
  • We do not sell your data and show no third-party advertising. Purchases are cosmetic or a one-time ad removal.

Information we collect

The campaign, your settings, earned cosmetics, Archive progress, and local replays work without an account and are stored only on your device until you delete them, reset your profile, or remove the Game (subject to your platform's backup behavior). In this version, gameplay analytics is off and no gameplay events are transmitted off your device.

We do not collect personal information

The Game has no account system and no login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any government identifier. You can play without providing any personal information.

Motion and sensors

THE LAST MIRROR uses your device's orientation and gyroscope sensors on your device only to aim the Mirror Seeker. Raw orientation, gyroscope, and gravity readings are never uploaded. We do not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or body sensors. A local motion diagnostic trace exists only for troubleshooting, requires an explicit action, is not uploaded by default, and can be deleted on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play using Unity IAP. When you buy an item, the Game sends a transient store receipt over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our first-party server, which asks the Google Play Developer API to confirm the purchase. We do not store the receipt or purchase token, and we never see or store your payment-card data — Google handles payment. Your game profile stores only the resulting entitlement (which item you own), not raw receipts. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, we remove the associated store entitlement while preserving cosmetics you earned through play. Store items are direct cosmetics (frames, cores, trails, capes, emblems, replay treatments) or a one-time ad removal; they do not sell damage, accuracy, boss weaknesses, leaderboard advantage, or Daily attempts.

Advertising and third parties

The Game shows no third-party advertising and embeds no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers, and we do not use an advertising identifier. The one-time ad-removal purchase suppresses the Game's own optional interstitial moments. Google Play processes your purchases as described above.

AI-assisted development

Some of the Game's art, textures, and audio were created with the help of AI-assisted tools during development, all reviewed by a human before shipping. No AI model or large language model runs inside the shipped Game, and players cannot prompt the Game to generate content. No personal information is used for any AI processing.

Children's privacy

THE LAST MIRROR is a puzzle-arcade boss game intended for teens and older; it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and security

On-device data lives only on your device until you remove it. The transient purchase receipt we send for verification is not retained after the purchase is confirmed. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit (encryption via HTTPS). We honor Google Play's Data Safety declarations for the Game.

Your choices and rights

Because the Game stores its data on your device, you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. If any optional online feature is enabled in a future version, it will use only a minimized or anonymous identifier, and you will be able to opt out and request export or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to processing of information about you; contact us to exercise them.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at kristine@dizzywalk.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
