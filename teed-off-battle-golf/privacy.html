Tee'd Off: Battle Golf — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective July 12, 2026 · Updated September 30, 2026

This policy explains how Dizzywalk handles information in Tee'd Off: Battle Golf and its related purchase, analytics and advertising services. Features vary by app version, region and your choices. Earlier offline-only versions do not transmit the information used by newer online features.

At a glance
  • Game progress, settings and replays are stored on your device.
  • Optional usage analytics uses a random install identifier. You can change this choice in the game's Privacy screen.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases and purchase access. Ad-supported reward features use Unity Authentication, AppLovin MAX and our reward-verification service.
  • Contact kristine@dizzywalk.com to ask about your information or request deletion. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not itself delete server records.

Information and how we use it

Progress and preferences

We store completed holes, scores, replays, inventory, cosmetic selections, tutorial progress, settings and privacy choices locally so the game can resume and retain your progress. Local rival comparisons do not require you to create a public profile.

Optional usage analytics

If you permit usage analytics, the game sends gameplay and navigation events, session information, app/build and device details, and install-attribution information to PostHog. Events are associated with a random install identifier rather than your name. This information is pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous. We use it to understand play, improve reliability and features, and measure how players find the game. The game does not ask for contacts, photos or precise GPS location for this purpose.

Declining usage analytics stops this optional event transmission. Withdrawing permission stops new transmission and clears queued events on the device. It does not automatically remove events already received; you can request their deletion as described below.

Purchases and restored access

Google Play processes purchase checkout. RevenueCat helps us validate transactions, associate purchase access with a generated game customer identifier, restore access and reconcile cancellations or refunds. This involves purchase history and transaction/product details, the generated identifier and technical app/device information. We use these records to provide purchased features, analyze purchases and prevent incorrect grants or fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase processing is separate from the optional usage-analytics choice.

The game's RevenueCat integration disables automatic advertising-identifier collection. This does not mean that an independently enabled advertising service collects no identifiers.

Advertising and verified rewards

When advertising features are enabled for an eligible player, AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners may process advertising or other device identifiers, IP/network information, app/device details, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and secure ads. IP information may also indicate an approximate region. Personalized advertising depends on applicable requirements and the choices available through the consent interface. Refusing personalization is not a promise that all data needed to deliver or secure an ad stops being processed.

Reward verification uses an automatically generated Unity Authentication player identifier and authenticated reward-attempt records. Our server receives signed ad-completion callbacks and stores the correlation, reward and settlement records needed to grant the displayed reward and prevent duplicate or fraudulent grants. This internal authentication does not require you to enter an email address or create a public login.

Where required, the advertising consent interface collects your choices before advertising requests are permitted. The game's Privacy screen provides access to available advertising privacy options. The current age controls prevent advertising initialization while the recorded age category is unknown or under 18. This describes the current product behavior; applicable child-privacy requirements still govern.

Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message and purchase reference. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, complete card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers for purchase validation, analytics, authentication, hosting and support. Advertising partners may process information for their own advertising purposes as described in their policies. Relevant providers include:

  • Google / Google Play — checkout and advertising-consent services.
  • RevenueCat — purchase processing and access management.
  • PostHog — optional usage analytics.
  • Unity — authentication for online reward features.
  • AppLovin — advertising and mediation.
  • Render — hosting our verification service and this policy.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and our services, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections. Providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Retention and security

We retain information for the time needed to operate the relevant feature, respond to requests, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable obligations. Purchase and verification records may need to be retained after an app is uninstalled or a deletion request is received. Local progress remains until you clear the app's data, uninstall it or otherwise remove it; platform backups may be controlled separately by your device settings.

Online requests use encrypted transport. We restrict access to operational records, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can change optional usage analytics and access available advertising privacy choices through the game's Privacy screen. Device and Google account settings may provide additional advertising and backup controls. Purchase management and refunds are handled through Google Play and applicable store procedures.

To request access, correction or deletion, email kristine@dizzywalk.com and identify Tee'd Off. Tell us whether the request concerns analytics, purchases or an ad reward. We may ask for enough information to locate the relevant records and verify the request. We will delete or de-identify eligible records, subject to legitimate retention requirements, and explain any applicable limitation. A data-deletion request does not automatically cancel a store purchase or subscription, or request a refund.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

Children's privacy

Advertising eligibility is controlled separately from gameplay. We do not permit the current advertising integration to initialize for an unknown or under-18 age category. If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and address it.
