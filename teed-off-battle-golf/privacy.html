Tee'd Off: Battle Golf — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective July 12, 2026 · Updated October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Dizzywalk handles information in Tee'd Off: Battle Golf and its related purchase, analytics, attribution and advertising services. Features vary by app version, region and your choices. Earlier offline-only versions do not transmit the information used by newer online features.

At a glance
  • Game progress, settings and replays are stored on your device.
  • Optional usage analytics uses a random install identifier. You can change this choice in the game's Privacy screen.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases and purchase access. Ad-supported reward features use Unity Authentication, AppLovin MAX and our reward-verification service.
  • Tenjin measures which ad campaign brought an install and the value of purchases and ads. It shares install, purchase, ad-revenue and milestone events, with your device's advertising identifier, with the ad networks we advertise with, currently Meta.
  • Contact kristine@dizzywalk.com to ask about your information or request deletion. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not itself delete server records.

Information and how we use it

Progress and preferences

We store completed holes, scores, replays, inventory, cosmetic selections, tutorial progress, settings and privacy choices locally so the game can resume and retain your progress. Local rival comparisons do not require you to create a public profile.

Optional usage analytics

If you permit usage analytics, the game sends gameplay and navigation events, session information, app/build and device details, and install-attribution information to PostHog. Events are associated with a random install identifier rather than your name. This information is pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous. We use it to understand play, improve reliability and features, and measure how players find the game. The game does not ask for contacts, photos or precise GPS location for this purpose.

Declining usage analytics stops this optional event transmission. Withdrawing permission stops new transmission and clears queued events on the device. It does not automatically remove events already received; you can request their deletion as described below.

Purchases and restored access

Google Play processes purchase checkout. RevenueCat helps us validate transactions, associate purchase access with a generated game customer identifier, restore access and reconcile cancellations or refunds. This involves purchase history and transaction/product details, the generated identifier and technical app/device information. We use these records to provide purchased features, analyze purchases and prevent incorrect grants or fraud. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase processing is separate from the optional usage-analytics choice.

The game's RevenueCat integration disables automatic advertising-identifier collection. This does not mean that an independently enabled advertising or attribution service collects no identifiers. When a paid order settles, its details are also sent to Tenjin for advertising measurement, as described under "Attribution and advertising measurement" below.

Advertising and verified rewards

When advertising features are enabled for an eligible player, AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners (currently Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Moloco, Pangle, Liftoff (Vungle), InMobi and ironSource) may process advertising or other device identifiers, IP/network information, app/device details, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and secure ads. IP information may also indicate an approximate region. Personalized advertising depends on applicable requirements and the choices available through the consent interface. Refusing personalization is not a promise that all data needed to deliver or secure an ad stops being processed.

Reward verification uses an automatically generated Unity Authentication player identifier and authenticated reward-attempt records. Our server receives signed ad-completion callbacks and stores the correlation, reward and settlement records needed to grant the displayed reward and prevent duplicate or fraudulent grants. This internal authentication does not require you to enter an email address or create a public login.

Where required, the advertising consent interface collects your choices before advertising requests are permitted. The game's Privacy screen provides access to available advertising privacy options. The current age controls prevent advertising initialization while the recorded age category is unknown or under 18. This describes the current product behavior; applicable child-privacy requirements still govern.

Attribution and advertising measurement (Tenjin)

We use Tenjin to measure installs and advertising performance, for example which ad campaign led to an install and how much purchase and ad revenue installs from that campaign produce. Tenjin receives your device's advertising identifier and similar device identifiers (such as the app set ID), the Google Play install referrer, device and app information, your IP address (which may indicate an approximate region), app-open events, a small set of value milestones (for example, when the purchases and ad revenue from an install pass a set amount within its first week), the details of a completed purchase (product, price, currency, order identifier and Google Play's signed purchase receipt, which Tenjin uses to validate the purchase) and the revenue of ads shown in the game. It does not receive your name, email address or payment-card details.

Tenjin shares these install, purchase, ad-revenue and milestone events, with the advertising identifier, with the ad networks we buy ads from, currently Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads), so they can attribute installs and measure and optimize our ad campaigns. Those networks process this information under their own privacy policies.

Tenjin starts only after Google's consent tool has answered, and only for players whose recorded age category is 18 or over, because it runs together with the advertising integration. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it starts only if you consented to storing or accessing information on your device; refusing keeps it off, and withdrawing that consent later through AD PRIVACY OPTIONS switches it to opted-out mode. When Tenjin starts, the game passes it your choices about the use of your data for ads and for ad personalization. Tenjin also does not start while you have declined optional usage analytics in the game's Privacy screen; a change made during play applies from the next time the game starts.

Age checks

Depending on the app version, your Google Play settings and applicable requirements, newer Android versions may request age-range and verification or approval signals from Google Play. Google Play may display its own age-sharing prompt. The game uses the returned signals locally for age-related compliance and child protection, including restricting advertising access when required verification is unresolved or a restrictive age signal is returned.

The returned Play Age Signals are not written to your saved game and are not sent to our advertising, analytics or attribution providers. They are not used for advertising targeting, personalization or commercial segmentation. Your manually selected age category is stored separately in local preferences. Required or unresolved checks can leave advertising unavailable while gameplay remains accessible. These checks are separate from optional usage analytics and advertising privacy choices.

Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message and purchase reference. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, complete card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers for purchase validation, analytics, attribution, authentication, hosting and support. Advertising partners may process information for their own advertising purposes as described in their policies. Through Tenjin we share install, purchase, ad-revenue and milestone events with the ad networks we advertise with, to measure and improve our advertising; depending on where you live, this may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under local law. You can limit it by deleting or resetting your device's advertising ID in your device settings, or by contacting us. Relevant providers include:

  • Google / Google Play — checkout, age-check and advertising-consent services.
  • RevenueCat — purchase processing and access management.
  • PostHog — optional usage analytics.
  • Unity — authentication for online reward features.
  • AppLovin — advertising and mediation, with the ad networks named under "Advertising and verified rewards".
  • Tenjin — install attribution and advertising measurement, including purchase validation for that measurement.
  • Meta — receives install, purchase, ad-revenue and milestone events from Tenjin to measure our ad campaigns, and supplies ads through Meta Audience Network.
  • Render — hosting our verification service and this policy.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and our services, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections. Providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Retention and security

We retain information for the time needed to operate the relevant feature, respond to requests, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable obligations. Purchase and verification records may need to be retained after an app is uninstalled or a deletion request is received. Local progress remains until you clear the app's data, uninstall it or otherwise remove it; platform backups may be controlled separately by your device settings.

Online requests use encrypted transport. We restrict access to operational records, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can change optional usage analytics and access available advertising privacy choices through the game's Privacy screen. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, AD PRIVACY OPTIONS also controls Tenjin attribution. Device and Google account settings, including deleting or resetting your advertising ID, may provide additional advertising, attribution and backup controls. Purchase management and refunds are handled through Google Play and applicable store procedures.

To request access, correction or deletion, email kristine@dizzywalk.com and identify Tee'd Off. Tell us whether the request concerns analytics, attribution, purchases or an ad reward. We may ask for enough information to locate the relevant records and verify the request. We will delete or de-identify eligible records, subject to legitimate retention requirements, and explain any applicable limitation. A data-deletion request does not automatically cancel a store purchase or subscription, or request a refund.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

Children's privacy

Advertising eligibility is controlled separately from gameplay. We do not permit the current advertising or attribution integrations to initialize for an unknown or under-18 age category. If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and address it.
