BAR FLIP — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-08-15 · Last updated: 2026-08-15

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game BAR FLIP (the "Game", package com.dizzywalk.barflip) on Google Play and related platforms. BAR FLIP is a rhyme-duel word game. This page describes exactly what the Game does and does not collect, and it was written by reading the shipping build rather than by describing an intention.

Short version.
  • No account and no login — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • This version of the Game transmits no data off your device at all. Your words, scores, streaks and settings stay on your device.
  • No advertising, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • We do not sell your data, and the Game never uploads the words you type.

Information we collect

In this version, we collect nothing. Your daily runs, scores, streaks, mastery progress, profile and settings are created and stored only on your device, and they stay there until you delete them, reset your profile, or remove the Game (subject to your platform's own backup behaviour). There is no server that receives them.

We do not collect personal information

The Game has no account system and no login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or any government identifier. You can play the entire Game without providing any personal information.

The words you type

BAR FLIP is played by typing words. Every word you type is judged on your device against a bundled pronunciation dictionary — nothing you type is sent anywhere for judging, scoring, or any other purpose. The Game deliberately holds this line in its sharing feature too: a share never carries the words you invented, only your score summary, and the Game says so on screen before anything is shared.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result, the Game hands a short line of text to your device's own share sheet or clipboard. You pick the destination, and nothing is sent until you do. We never see the share and we receive nothing from it.

Network access

The Game declares the standard Android INTERNET permission. In this version no shipped feature uses it to transmit anything: the leaderboard client bundled in this build is the offline one, which reports rank and percentile as unknown and displays "—" rather than inventing a number, and the ghost opponents you play against are bundled recordings shipped inside the app, clearly labelled GHOST · SEED, not live players. The Game never disguises a recorded ghost or a bot as a live human.

Analytics

The Game contains a first-party analytics transport that is disabled and unconfigured in this build: with no analytics key present, the transport is never created and no events are generated or sent. There is no third-party analytics SDK embedded in the Game. If a future version enables analytics, it would collect pseudonymous gameplay events (such as run starts and completions) and never the words you type; we would update this page and the Play Data Safety declaration before that version ships.

Advertising and third parties

The Game shows no advertising and embeds no third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs. We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers, and the Game does not use an advertising identifier.

Purchases

This version contains no in-app purchases and no billing integration — the shipping app requests no billing permission and offers nothing for sale. If purchases are added in a future version, they would be processed by Google Play, we would never see or store your payment-card data, and this page would be updated before that version ships.

AI-assisted development

Some of the Game's art and audio were created with the help of AI-assisted tools during development, all reviewed by a human before shipping. No AI model or large language model runs inside the shipped Game, and none is used to judge your words: rhymes are scored by a deterministic phoneme engine over the Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary, so the same word in the same position always scores the same. Players cannot prompt the Game to generate content, and no personal information is used for any AI processing.

Children's privacy

BAR FLIP is a word game with a stylised bar-room setting, intended for teens and older; it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — and in this version we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Data retention and security

On-device data lives only on your device until you remove it. Because this version transmits nothing, there is no server-side copy of your data to retain, breach, or disclose. We honour Google Play's Data Safety declarations for the Game.

Your choices and rights

Because the Game stores its data on your device, you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. If any optional online feature is enabled in a future version, it will use only a minimised or anonymous identifier, and you will be able to opt out and request export or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to processing of information about you; contact us to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
