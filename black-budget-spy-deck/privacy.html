SPIREBOUND: Soul & Blade — Privacy Policy

Published by DIZZYWALK RECORDS LLC (Dizzywalk)

Effective date: 2026-11-08 · Last updated: 2026-11-08 · Android, Google Play, app identifier com.blackbudget.operation (formerly listed as Black Budget)

Short version. There is no account or sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game shows rewarded, interstitial, banner and app-open ads through AppLovin MAX and its ad partners, including Google AdMob; sells one-time items and a 30-day pass through Google Play; measures gameplay and revenue with PostHog; sends crash diagnostics to Sentry; and measures which advertising campaign led to an install with Tenjin. Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) checks on every launch whether your consent is needed. Where it is, as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it asks for your choices before ads start: ads are personalised only with your consent, and analytics and attribution run only if you agree to information being stored on your device. Elsewhere ads, analytics and attribution start when the game opens.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress, currencies, purchases and the record of what each purchase granted, settings and accessibility preferences
  • A random player identifier created by the game (not your advertising ID or your name)
  • Ad-pacing counters, the value milestone described below, and scheduled local reminders
  • Your consent choices, stored by Google's consent tool in the industry-standard IAB format so the game and its ad partners can read them

Data we collect and why

Advertising (AppLovin MAX and partners)

AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it works with can process your Android advertising ID, app identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, privacy signals, and ad requests, impressions, clicks and technical performance information, to show, measure and limit ads, credit rewarded ads, improve their services and prevent fraud. The game contains adapters for Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), Meta Audience Network, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Mintegral, Moloco, Chartboost and ironSource (Unity LevelPlay) alongside AppLovin's own services. Google AdMob, for example, collects your IP address, interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, performance information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use, and your advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The game gives MAX its random player identifier. The ad revenue MAX reports for each ad you see is recorded in our analytics and passed to Tenjin.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

On every launch the game asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether your consent is needed. Where it is, Google's consent message appears before any ad is requested, and where Google's tool requires a way to review your choices, Settings → PRIVACY opens Google's privacy choices form. To do this Google receives your IP address, basic device and app information and the choices you make; the choices are stored on your device and passed to ad partners as described above. Until Google's tool has answered on a launch, no ads are requested and no analytics or attribution data is sent, except on the basis of the answer you gave on an earlier launch.

Gameplay analytics (PostHog)

Predefined events about sessions, the tutorial, floors and fights started and finished, chute drops, the store, purchases (product, price and currency), ads requested, shown and completed with their reported revenue, and changes to in-game currencies. Events carry the build, the random player identifier, your device's country code (from the SIM, network or language setting) and, when Tenjin reports it, the install campaign, ad network and organic/paid source; the event that records the consent answer also carries the device's time zone. No name, email, advertising ID, purchase token or free text is sent. When the game loads its current settings (such as ad pacing) from PostHog it sends the random player identifier. PostHog receives the IP address of the connection, as with any web request.

Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

When the game hits an error, Sentry receives the error type, where in the code it happened, the build and the time, plus basic device details. Sentry receives the IP address of the connection, as with any web request.

Install attribution (Tenjin)

Tenjin measures which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and the purchases, ad revenue and one value milestone associated with it (the milestone is sent once when the value an install has generated in its first week reaches a set level; the game asks our server for the level that applies to your country code, a request that carries only that code and, as with any web request, the IP address of the connection). It can process your Android advertising ID, Android App Set ID, the Google Play install referrer, your IP address and the approximate country derived from it, basic device details, the random player identifier, and purchase data signed by Google Play (product, price, currency, order reference). Depending on our campaign connections, Tenjin can report the install and those events to the advertising network that ran the campaign.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The game checks the purchase data that Google Play signs on your device and records the product, a receipt reference and what was granted in the save on your device. Settings → RESTORE re-checks what you own with Google Play. The 30-day pass does not renew by itself.

Device country

The game reads your device's country (SIM, network or language setting) and time zone. The country sets how often interstitial ads may appear in your region (AppLovin's own country check is used first when it is available) and which value milestone level applies, and both are included in analytics as described above. Neither decides whether consent is needed: Google's consent tool does. Until it has answered, AppLovin's own region check keeps ads non-personalised where it reports GDPR rules apply.

Ads in the game

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a stated in-game reward, and the reward is granted only when the ad network confirms the ad completed. Interstitial ads appear at natural breaks once the guided first fight is done, such as a fight's result, a defeat, a chapter's result and the return Home, never at launch, at the start of a fight or during play. App-open ads can appear when the game opens or when you return to it, and a banner can appear at the top of the screen from the first floor. Players who have made a purchase see no interstitial, banner or app-open ads; optional rewarded ads stay available. How often ads appear is set by the current game configuration.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent message asks before ads start. Ads are personalised only if you consent to personalised advertising; otherwise only non-personalised ads are requested. Analytics and attribution data are sent only if you agree to information being stored on and read from your device. Change your choices at any time in Settings → PRIVACY.
  • Wherever Google's consent tool offers privacy choices, Settings → PRIVACY opens them; elsewhere it opens this page.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). With no advertising ID, partners cannot use it for personalised ads or campaign measurement.
  • To opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or targeted advertising under US state law, or to ask us to stop analytics for your install, email us (below) with your device model and roughly when you play.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Letting advertising and attribution partners use your advertising ID for personalised ads or campaign measurement can count as "selling" or "sharing" under some US state laws.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • PostHog, Sentry and Tenjin: only as long as needed to improve, measure and fix the game, within each provider's retention period for our account.
  • Advertising and consent data: under the policies of AppLovin, Google and their partners. Google Play keeps its own order records.

Data deletion

There is no account; progress and purchase records live on your device and we hold no game profile on our own servers. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling, removes the data on your device. To ask us to delete analytics, crash or attribution data held by our providers, email mtime88@gmail.com with the subject SPIREBOUND data deletion request and include what you have: a Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) for a purchase, roughly when you played, and your device model. We delete the records we can identify and ask our providers to do the same where applicable. Don't send passwords, card details or purchase tokens.

Children

SPIREBOUND is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data we can identify.
