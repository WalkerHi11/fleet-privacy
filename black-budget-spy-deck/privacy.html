SPIREBOUND: Soul & Blade — Privacy Policy

Published by DIZZYWALK RECORDS LLC (Dizzywalk)

Effective date: 2026-10-20 · Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Android, Google Play, app identifier com.blackbudget.operation (formerly listed as Black Budget)

Short version. There is no account or sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game shows rewarded, interstitial, banner and app-open ads through AppLovin MAX and its ad partners; sells one-time items and a 30-day pass through Google Play; measures gameplay and revenue with PostHog; sends crash diagnostics to Sentry; and measures which advertising campaign led to an install with Tenjin. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game shows only non-personalised ads and sends no analytics or attribution data. Elsewhere ads, analytics and attribution start when the game opens.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress, currencies, purchases and the record of what each purchase granted, settings and accessibility preferences
  • A random player identifier created by the game (not your advertising ID or your name)
  • Ad-pacing counters, the value milestone described below, and scheduled local reminders

Data we collect and why

Advertising (AppLovin MAX and partners)

AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it works with can process your Android advertising ID, app identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, privacy signals, and ad requests, impressions, clicks and technical performance information, to show, measure and limit ads, credit rewarded ads, improve their services and prevent fraud. The game contains adapters for Meta Audience Network, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Mintegral and Moloco alongside AppLovin's own services. The game gives MAX its random player identifier. The ad revenue MAX reports for each ad you see is recorded in our analytics and passed to Tenjin.

Gameplay analytics (PostHog)

Predefined events about sessions, the tutorial, floors and fights started and finished, chute drops, the store, purchases (product, price and currency), ads requested, shown and completed with their reported revenue, and changes to in-game currencies. Events carry the build, the random player identifier and, when Tenjin reports it, the install campaign, ad network and organic/paid source. No name, email, advertising ID, purchase token or free text is sent. When the game loads its current settings (such as ad pacing) from PostHog it sends the random player identifier. PostHog receives the IP address of the connection, as with any web request.

Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

When the game hits an error, Sentry receives the error type, where in the code it happened, the build and the time, plus basic device details. Sentry receives the IP address of the connection, as with any web request.

Install attribution (Tenjin)

Tenjin measures which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and the purchases, ad revenue and one value milestone associated with it (the milestone is sent once when the value an install has generated in its first week reaches a set level). It can process your Android advertising ID, Android App Set ID, the Google Play install referrer, your IP address and the approximate country derived from it, basic device details, the random player identifier, and purchase data signed by Google Play (product, price, currency, order reference). Depending on our campaign connections, Tenjin can report the install and those events to the advertising network that ran the campaign.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The game checks the purchase data that Google Play signs on your device and records the product, a receipt reference and what was granted in the save on your device. Settings → RESTORE re-checks what you own with Google Play. The 30-day pass does not renew by itself.

Region check

The game reads your device's country (SIM, network or language setting) and time zone, on the device only, to tell whether you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. AppLovin's own geography check is used the same way.

Ads in the game

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a stated in-game reward, and the reward is granted only when the ad network confirms the ad completed. Interstitial ads appear at natural breaks after the first chapter, such as a fight's result, a chapter's result and the return Home, never at launch, at the start of a fight or during play. App-open ads can appear when the game opens or when you return to it, and a banner can appear at the top of the screen from the first floor. Players who have made a purchase see no interstitial, banner or app-open ads; optional rewarded ads stay available. How often ads appear is set by the current game configuration.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: only non-personalised ads are requested and no analytics or attribution data is sent.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). With no advertising ID, partners cannot use it for personalised ads or campaign measurement.
  • To opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or targeted advertising under US state law, or to ask us to stop analytics for your install, email us (below) with your device model and roughly when you play.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Letting advertising and attribution partners use your advertising ID for personalised ads or campaign measurement can count as "selling" or "sharing" under some US state laws.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • PostHog, Sentry and Tenjin: only as long as needed to improve, measure and fix the game, within each provider's retention period for our account.
  • Advertising data: under the policies of AppLovin and its partners. Google Play keeps its own order records.

Data deletion

There is no account; progress and purchase records live on your device and we hold no game profile on our own servers. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling, removes the data on your device. To ask us to delete analytics, crash or attribution data held by our providers, email mtime88@gmail.com with the subject SPIREBOUND data deletion request and include what you have: a Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) for a purchase, roughly when you played, and your device model. We delete the records we can identify and ask our providers to do the same where applicable. Don't send passwords, card details or purchase tokens.

Children

SPIREBOUND is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data we can identify.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use these rights.
