Thread Heist: Unravel & Escape — Privacy Policy

Developer: Dizzywalk · Effective: 2026-10-20 · Last updated: 2026-11-10

This policy explains what information the game Thread Heist: Unravel & Escape (the "Game") on Google Play handles, why, and the choices you have.

Short version

  • You do not need an account, name, email, contacts, photos or precise location to play.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • Ads are served through AppLovin MAX. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form appears before any ad is requested; the ad SDK is not started before then.
  • We use Tenjin for install attribution and PostHog for anonymous gameplay analytics. For some players, Meta's app events SDK tells Meta when the Game is opened, so our ads on Facebook and Instagram reach people who play.
  • Google Play processes payments. We never see your card details.

Information we collect

  • On your device: level progress, coins, gems, boosters, settings and similar game state. It stays on the device unless you reinstall or clear app data.
  • Analytics (PostHog): an anonymous, randomly generated player identifier, gameplay events (for example level start, level complete, level failed, purchases started), app version, device model and OS version, and coarse diagnostics. No name, email or precise location.
  • Attribution (Tenjin): the advertising identifier where available, install referrer and campaign information, selected in-game events, purchase details (product, price, currency and Google Play's signed purchase record) and ad-impression revenue reported by the ad mediation SDK, so we can tell which campaigns bring players.
  • Meta (Facebook SDK app events): for a share of players, rolled out gradually, the Game includes Meta's app events SDK (facebook-core only: no Facebook login or sharing). It sends Meta that the app was installed and opened, with device information, app version and, where your consent and device settings allow, the advertising identifier, so Meta can measure and optimise our ads. It sends no purchases, gameplay or ad-revenue events. Where the law requires consent (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland) it starts only after you agree in the consent form; if you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under a US state law, Meta's Limited Data Use applies and the advertising identifier is not sent. Players without it send the same app-open signal through Tenjin instead.
  • Advertising (AppLovin MAX and its partners, including Google): device and advertising identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, consent signals, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics. The Game does not request precise location.
  • Session replay (Fleet Replay): for a sampled share of players, the Game uses Fleet Replay, Dizzywalk's own session-replay service, to find confusing steps and broken screens. When it runs it sends a reduced-size still image of what the Game itself draws about every 3 seconds (not video and not audio; at most 720 pixels on the long edge), where you tapped, and a reviewed subset of the gameplay events described above (for example level start, complete or fail, rewarded-ad offers and purchase steps), linked to the same random analytics player identifier (never your name, email, advertising ID, payment details or location). Full-screen ads, the Google Play purchase sheet, system screens and other apps are never recorded. It follows the same choice as our analytics (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only after you agree in the consent form) and can be turned off remotely. Recordings go over HTTPS to our Fleet Replay service, hosted by Render in the United States, are not shared with other companies, and are deleted after 14 days.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles payment. The Game receives the product, the purchase token and the purchase status needed to deliver what you bought.

Advertising, analytics and consent

  • SDKs: Fleet Replay (Dizzywalk session replay), AppLovin MAX (with Google Mobile Ads mediation), the AppLovin consent flow with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), Tenjin, PostHog, Meta app events (facebook-core, for a share of players) and Unity In-App Purchasing (Google Play Billing).
  • When ads start: a new player first plays the opening level, which shows no ads. The ad SDK and its consent form start afterwards, before any ad is requested.
  • Your ad choices: where required, the consent form lets you accept or decline personalised ads. You can reopen the privacy choices from the Game's Settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Provider policies: AppLovin, Google, Tenjin, PostHog, Meta.

How we use information

  • To run the Game, save progress and deliver purchases.
  • To understand how levels play (where players get stuck, crash or leave) and improve the Game.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free, and to measure which campaigns bring players.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above, for the purposes above, and where the law requires.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

Retention and security

  • Game data on your device stays until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Analytics and attribution records are kept by the providers only as long as needed for the purposes above, and purchase records as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention, accounting and the law.
  • The Game sends data over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear the Game's data or uninstall it to remove on-device data.
  • Use the Game's Settings to reopen the ad privacy choices, and your device settings to limit ad tracking.
  • To ask for access to or deletion of data we hold, email kristine@dizzywalk.com with the subject "Thread Heist data request". Requests about data held by AppLovin, Google, Tenjin, PostHog or Meta can also go to those providers directly.
