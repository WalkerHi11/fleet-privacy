Brink Blitz — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-12 · Last updated: 2026-10-30

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information for Brink Blitz (Android package com.dizzywalk.brinkblitz) on Google Play and related platforms. Capabilities depend on your installed version: the older web-wrapper Android release (version code 2) stored progress locally on your device and did not include Unity billing, advertising, or attribution SDKs. Native Unity releases starting with version code 304 (v1.2.0+) include Google Play Billing via Unity In-App Purchasing, AppLovin MAX advertising mediation with Google UMP consent, PostHog gameplay analytics, Tenjin attribution, Firebase Analytics, Sentry crash diagnostics, and optional local reminders as described below.

At a glance
  • No account login required: You can play without providing your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.
  • Privacy choices & consent: Where required by law (including the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form appears before personalized ads or measurement run, and you can update your choices anytime in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Purchases & subscriptions: Google Play processes payments; we never receive your payment-card details. Subscriptions (Weekly VIP) can be managed or canceled anytime in Settings → Manage Subscription or Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Local gameplay saves: Ordinary earned progress and settings are saved locally on your device.

1. Consent and your privacy choices

On native Unity releases (version code 304+), the game checks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) at startup to determine whether regional privacy consent is required (such as in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland). Where required, the consent form is shown before personalized advertising or measurement is enabled. You can reopen privacy options at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android's system privacy settings.

2. Gameplay analytics and crash diagnostics

PostHog (us.i.posthog.com): Unity releases send structured gameplay, progression, store, and ad lifecycle events (such as session starts, drive outcomes, upgrade sinks, ad impressions, and verified purchase settlements) tied to a random app-scoped identifier (distinct_id). We do not use PostHog session recording or web tracking cookies. Where regional consent is required and declined, analytics transmission is suppressed. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Google Firebase Analytics: Measures app lifecycle, progression milestones, verified purchases, and ad-revenue events using Google Consent Mode signals derived from your UMP choice. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Sentry (sentry.io): To diagnose crashes and maintain stability, Unity releases send crash stack traces, unhandled error diagnostics, session-health counts, device model, OS version, and build version against a random installation identifier, with default PII collection disabled and no screenshots attached. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (AppLovin MAX & mediation partners)

Unity releases (version code 304+) use AppLovin MAX to serve optional rewarded video ads for stated in-game rewards, natural-break interstitial ads between drives, bottom banner ads (for non-exempt players), and app-open/resume ads. Integrated mediation networks may include AppLovin, Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral, InMobi, Pangle, Moloco, BidMachine, IronSource, Chartboost, and DT Exchange.

Advertising SDKs may process the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address / coarse network location, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention according to your consent status. Partner privacy policies: AppLovin, Google, Meta, Unity, and Liftoff.

4. Attribution and ad-revenue measurement (Tenjin)

Unity releases use Tenjin and the Google Play Install Referrer API to measure campaign attribution, install milestones, verified purchases, and impression-level ad revenue, applying your consent signals before connecting. See Tenjin's Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases, subscriptions, and receipt verification

Unity releases (version code 304+) use Unity In-App Purchasing (5.4+) and Google Play Billing for optional one-time bundles, gem packs, season passes, and an auto-renewing Weekly VIP subscription (vip_weekly). Google Play processes all payments and never shares your payment-card details with us. To verify purchases, prevent duplicate grants, and support restoration, the app sends the product identifier, Google Play purchase token, and package name over HTTPS to our commerce verifier (brink-blitz-ghost-api.onrender.com), which validates purchase status with the Google Play Developer API before granting paid entitlements. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Local notifications and permissions

On Android 13 and newer, the game may ask for notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) so it can schedule optional local reminders on your device (for example when a timed Supply Crate is ready). You can turn notifications off at any time in Android Settings. The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera, photos, or precise GPS location.

7. Children's privacy

Brink Blitz is intended for general/teen audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

8. Data retention, your rights, and deletion requests

On-device progress and settings remain in private app storage until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Server-side purchase-verification records and aggregate analytics are retained only as long as needed for fraud/replay prevention, accounting, and product improvement.

To request access to or deletion of analytics or purchase-verification records associated with your installation, open Settings in the game to view your short Support ID (or include a Google Play order number such as GPA....) and email kristine@dizzywalk.com with the subject Brink Blitz data deletion request. Do not email payment-card details.

9. Contact and changes

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email Dizzywalk at kristine@dizzywalk.com. When we update how the game handles information, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
