Dizzywalk Auto — Privacy Policy

Operated by Dizzywalk Records

Effective date: 2026-10-19

Dizzywalk Auto is an internal tool that Dizzywalk Records uses to manage its own advertising on Meta's platforms. It is not offered to the public and has no end users outside our team.

What it processes

Through the Meta Marketing API it reads and changes advertising objects (campaigns, ad sets, ads and creatives) in ad accounts that Dizzywalk Records owns, and reads their aggregated performance reports (spend, impressions, clicks, installs and in-app events). It does not collect personal data about the people who see our ads, and it does not use Facebook Login.

Why

To report on, launch and manage our own advertising.

Where it is kept

In our own database, hosted by our infrastructure provider, and accessible only to our team and the tools they use to run our advertising. We keep reports for as long as we need them to evaluate our campaigns.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with third parties, apart from service providers that host or process it on our behalf.

Deletion

We delete the data on request from Meta or when we stop using the tool. To ask about or request deletion, contact kristine@dizzywalk.com.
