Pull Ya To Chat? — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-12 · Last updated: 2026-09-05

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in Pull Ya To Chat? (the "Game") on Google Play and related platforms.

Short version.
  • You can play without an account, name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • The current Android candidate uses Google User Messaging Platform consent controls before AppLovin MAX advertising, plus consent-gated Tenjin attribution and anonymous PostHog product analytics.
  • Google Play processes payments. We receive only the purchase information needed to verify, restore, refund, or revoke an entitlement; we do not receive payment-card details.

Information we collect

The Game stores progress, settings, and earned state on your device. Online leaderboard features use a randomly generated player identifier, a signed session token, a display name, and gameplay score and replay data. You may replace the generated display name; do not use personal information in it, because leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players.

With your in-game analytics consent, we process anonymous game activity, coarse device capability, app diagnostics, and, where present, advertising click identifiers from the link that opened the Game. PostHog receives the consented analytics events. On native Android, Tenjin receives consented attribution and selected in-game events. When an AppLovin MAX ad is displayed, the Game sends the ad impression and revenue metadata to Tenjin for attribution and revenue measurement.

When advertising is available, AppLovin MAX and its configured ad-network partners may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location, consent signals, ad interactions, diagnostics, and impression-level revenue for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, and measurement. The Game does not request precise location.

When you make or restore a purchase, the Game processes the product identifier, Google Play purchase token, anonymous player identifier and entitlement status with its verification service. This supports purchase verification, acknowledgement, restoration, refunds, revocation, fraud prevention, and accounting. Google Play handles your payment details.

Advertising, analytics, and consent

The Android candidate includes AppLovin MAX, with an optional Google Mobile Ads mediation adapter, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), Tenjin, and a direct PostHog analytics connection. On Android, UMP is the authority for whether the Game may initialize MAX; if UMP fails or does not allow ad requests, MAX stays off. MAX is currently initialized only in non-personalized mode. In-game analytics consent separately controls PostHog and Tenjin. The Settings screen provides UMP privacy options where required and lets you withdraw in-game analytics consent. These choices affect future collection and do not erase data already processed by a provider.

Provider policies are available at AppLovin Privacy, Tenjin Privacy, PostHog Privacy, and Google Privacy.

Online features and sharing

The leaderboard service processes leaderboard identifiers, display names, score and replay data on Dizzywalk's behalf. It uses salted hashes for stored player and rate-limit identifiers. Network services necessarily receive request metadata such as IP address to operate and protect the service. We share consented analytics, attribution, and advertising data with the providers described above, and purchase information with Google Play and the verification services needed to deliver the purchase.

Children's privacy

Pull Ya To Chat? is intended for teens and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete information we hold where applicable.

Data retention and security

On-device data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Provider and purchase-verification records may be retained as needed for restoration, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal obligations, subject to the relevant provider policy. We use HTTPS for hosted requests.

Your choices and rights

You can clear local Game data by clearing app data or uninstalling. The Settings screen lets you withdraw in-game analytics consent and open Google's ad privacy options when required. To request access to or deletion of data held by Dizzywalk, email kristine@dizzywalk.com with the subject “Pull Ya To Chat data deletion request” and your leaderboard display name or relevant transaction details. We will delete data we hold unless retention is necessary for fraud prevention, refunds, accounting, legal obligations, or to resolve a dispute. Requests concerning data held by a third-party provider are also governed by that provider's policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at kristine@dizzywalk.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
