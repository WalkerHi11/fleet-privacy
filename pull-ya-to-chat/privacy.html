Pull Ya To Chat? — Privacy Policy

Published by Dizzywalk

Effective date: 2026-07-12 · Last updated: 2026-08-14

This Privacy Policy explains how Dizzywalk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the game Pull Ya To Chat? (the "Game") on Google Play and related platforms. We built Pull Ya To Chat? to be fun without harvesting your personal life. This page describes exactly what the Game does and does not collect.

Short version.
  • You can play the default-dark configuration without creating an account or providing your name, email, or precise location.
  • The Android package includes AppLovin MAX, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and RevenueCat. Advertising and purchase surfaces remain disabled until separately authorized.
  • If advertising is enabled, UMP presents required consent and privacy choices before MAX may request ads. We do not sell personal data.

Information we collect

The Game stores progress, settings, and earned cosmetic/reward state on your device. Online leaderboard features use a randomly generated player identifier and signed session token. If an authorized release enables advertising, MAX and configured mediation partners may process advertising or device identifiers, approximate region, consent signals, ad interactions, diagnostics, and impression-level revenue for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, and measurement.

If an authorized release enables purchases, RevenueCat and the Game's verification service process the anonymous player identifier, product identifier, transaction/receipt information, and entitlement status to validate purchases, prevent replay, restore ownership, process refunds or revocations, and meet accounting obligations. We do not receive payment-card details.

Advertising, consent, and third parties

The Android package embeds AppLovin MAX and Google UMP, even while advertising is disabled. Before MAX may request an ad in an authorized release, UMP must complete its applicable regional consent and privacy-options flow. Your UMP choices are passed through the provider consent framework; completing a form does not itself mean you chose personalized advertising. The compiled mediation stack includes Google, Unity, ironSource, Meta, Mintegral, Liftoff/Unity, DT Exchange, and Bigo adapters; an authorized ad request may use any configured partner. RevenueCat is used for purchase and entitlement verification when purchases are enabled. Anonymous product analytics are off until the default-key consent decision is granted; when enabled, PostHog receives anonymous usage events and device/app diagnostics. Provider policies are available at AppLovin Privacy, Google Privacy, RevenueCat Privacy, and PostHog Privacy.

Online features

Daily ladder and rank standings use an anonymous, randomly generated player id and transmit in-game score/replay data, never your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Children's privacy

Pull Ya To Chat? is intended for teens and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

On-device data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Where advertising or purchases are enabled, provider and verification records may be retained as needed for restoration, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal obligations; provider retention is governed by the linked provider policies. We use HTTPS in transit for hosted requests.

Your choices and rights

You can clear local game data by clearing app data or uninstalling. Where required, the in-game Privacy choices entry opens the Google UMP privacy-options form to review or change advertising choices. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information we hold; some transaction records may need to be retained where legally required. We honor the Google Play Data Safety declaration for the released build.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at kristine@dizzywalk.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
