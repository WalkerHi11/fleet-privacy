Account and data deletion

Developer: Dizzywalk (DIZZYWALK RECORDS LLC). This page applies to the Google Play app com.dizzywalk.drownward.

Request deletion without reinstalling the app

  1. Email mtime88@gmail.com with the subject Drownward account and data deletion.
  2. Say that you want your Drownward online wallet account and its associated data deleted. Include your wallet account ID if you have it. If you also want records held by analytics, attribution or advertising providers deleted, say so and include any installation or support identifier available to you.
  3. We may need to verify that the account or records belong to you before completing your request. If you no longer have the app, account ID or recovery code, explain that in your email so we can help identify the records. Do not send your private recovery code, wallet authentication secret, password or payment-card details in the initial request.

You may request deletion of other personal data without deleting your wallet. Specify which records you want deleted. Records already sent to service providers are not automatically erased by deleting the wallet; request help using the same contact above.

Delete your online wallet yourself

If you have your private recovery code, use the online wallet deletion form, enter the code, confirm the permanent loss of unused paid credits and supplies, and select Delete paid wallet permanently. That form requires JavaScript; this information page and the email request route do not.

In the app, open The Shelf → Recovery → Delete paid wallet and confirm. Finish pending Google Play purchases first. If a purchase is still settling, reconnect in the game and retry after settlement, or use the email request above. A temporary outage of the online form does not prevent you from sending a deletion request.

What account deletion removes

  • The online wallet account and its authentication and recovery credentials; its recovery code stops working.
  • Unused paid credits and supplies, wallet-operation and online reward records.
  • Remaining Manifest claims and saved cards, online Workshop attempts and reward receipts, and Recovery Dispatch records.
  • Associated purchase-measurement delivery records and other nonfinancial wallet-linked records.

Self-service wallet deletion takes effect when the online form or app confirms completion. Email requests can require ownership verification and provider coordination; an instant completion time is not promised.

What is retained, and for how long

Purchase and refund records: minimal transaction records are retained after wallet deletion for transaction accounting, refunds and prevention of duplicate purchase claims. The wallet-account link is removed. These records include hashed transaction identifiers, purchase/refund facts, and subscription payment, enrollment and benefit-claim records retained under a replacement identifier, not your wallet authentication or recovery credentials. There is currently no automatic expiry or fixed deletion deadline for these retained records; they remain stored indefinitely unless separately removed.

Data on your device: earned salvage, dive progress and settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall, subject to Android backup/restore behavior. Deleting the online wallet does not clear this local data.

Provider-held data: analytics, attribution, advertising and ordinary hosting logs already received by providers are not automatically deleted by wallet deletion. Their retention depends on the provider and applicable requirements; there is no single fixed period for every provider. Use the email route above to request deletion of identifiable records. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations, transaction accuracy, security or disputes. See the Drownward privacy policy for the providers and the data they handle.

Deleting your wallet is not a refund request and does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage subscriptions and refund requests through Google Play separately.
